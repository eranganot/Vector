# Synthetic data plan — VECTOR Retail Group

Status: **Approved (Phase 1, 2026-10-04); updated for Phase 2 as built** (seed `p2-v4`, Eran's G2 scope). All data is
synthetic (charter §17). Code: `src/infra/seed/` (`org.ts`, `generator.ts`, `catalog.ts`, `seed.ts`).

## Principles

1. **Deterministic.** One seeded generator (`seed = "vector-v1"`): every value depends only on (branch or KPI, day,
   interventions), never on generation order, so every reset produces identical data and future days generated by the
   scenario engine are reproducible.
2. **Plausible to a retail executive.** Israeli retail calendar and geography, store size classes, weekday shape,
   holidays, noise. Investors notice fake-looking data immediately.
3. **Planted stories are ground truth.** Every condition a demo relies on is injected deliberately, with a known cause,
   as named constants in `generator.ts` (`P2S1`, `CATALOG_PLANTS`, the department plants). Tests check that each one is
   present and that the detector raises nothing else (no false alerts on 60 branches of noise).
4. **Fictional organization, real places.** Branch cities and coordinates are real (needed for live weather in Phase 6).
   Names of people and the company are invented.

## Generator model (per branch, per day)

```text
expected = base(size class) × region factor × branch factor × weekday shape × holiday factor × trend(t)
sales    = expected × (1 − 2.2 × OSA shortfall) × (1 + noise)
```

- **Base:** L ₪190k/day, M ₪110k/day; region factors North 0.97 · Coast 1.00 · Center 1.05 · Jerusalem 0.98 · South 0.92;
  a fixed per-branch factor 0.92–1.08.
- **Weekday shape** (net sales): Sun 0.95 · Mon 0.9 · Tue 0.9 · Wed 1.0 · Thu 1.25 · Fri 1.1 (short day) · Sat 0.35.
- **Holidays** (September–October 2026 in `src/domain/calendar.ts`): eves spike ×1.45; holy days trade at 5% (most
  branches closed). Passover and the rest of the year arrive with the 52-week history.
- **Noise:** Gaussian, σ = 4% of daily sales. Correlations are kept: transactions follow sales (basket ₪150–180), labor %
  rises when sales fall, OSA shortfalls cut sales the same day.
- Extra KPIs (shrinkage, NPS) draw from their own random streams, so adding them never changed the original four.

## Planted stories

**P2-S1, live (R8).** Haifa Grand Canyon. A DC routing change on 11 October dropped two top categories from its delivery
wave. On-shelf availability falls from 12 October, ramping over four days to ~89%; sales fall with it (−18% week on week by
the story day, 22 October). If an inventory transfer executes, availability recovers over three days and sales follow. The
detector finds it on the story day; nothing else in the 60 branches crosses its thresholds.

## Epochs

`pnpm demo:reset` (and the Demo controls' Reset) starts a new **organization epoch**: a new organization row with the full
seed, the detector run and the catalog. The previous epoch is deactivated, never deleted, so its audit history stays intact
(ADR-004). On deploy, `demo:reset --if-empty` reseeds only when no active epoch carries the current `SEED_VERSION`.
